## TONE & VOICE GUIDE

**Sound like:** A builder. Direct. Confident. Internet-native. Not a consultant.

**Do:**
- Short sentences. No filler words.
- Lead with outcomes, not process.
- Put numbers everywhere.
- Use strong, specific language ("5-7 days," "70,000 followers in 4 months," "85B+ views").
- Reference culture, not just marketing.
- Make clients feel like they're getting the inside track.

**Don't:**
- Corporate speak ("leverage synergies," "holistic solutions," "robust strategy").
- Vague promises without proof.
- Long paragraphs.
- Over-explain — hook first, details after.
- Passive voice.

**Sample phrases that work:**
- "Built to perform."
- "Looks are cool. Conversions are cooler."
- "Campaigns become systems."
- "We don't flex. They do it for us."
- "Fast, human, and built exactly how the internet likes it."
- "Hand us the keys."
- "Content that hits everywhere."
